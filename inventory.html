Before you talk to us

Where does a unit record start, and how do changes reach your listings?

What to bring

  1. Your source of record for inventory (DMS, feed or spreadsheet)
  2. One sample unit with its full record, photos and price
  3. The marketplaces and sites where your units appear today

What to verify

  1. Change a price or status on a test unit and confirm when and where it updates
  2. Add or reorder photos and check the result on each destination
  3. Confirm support for each marketplace you use, since requirements differ by marketplace
