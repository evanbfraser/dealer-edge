How we got this

The lifts come from the same chain we show across the platform — not invented multipliers:

  • Visitors ×1.25 — a 99-score site, AI content, AI ads and SEO bring more qualified buyers (DealerEdge Marketing).
  • Leads ×1.75 — 78% of dealer sites fail Core Web Vitals (avg. mobile load 17.3s, 200-site audit). A fast site plus 24/7 capture stops the bounce.
  • Showings ×3.2 — only 19 of 1,000 buyers ever book a showing today; fixing response (57% never reply in 24h) and voicemail (80% of voicemail callers call a competitor) recovers that to ~60. Same buyers.
  • Boats sold +50% — the dollar figure rests on this one alone. Pied Piper ILE Study: dealers who raise their response score (<40 → >80) sell 50% more units from the same leads. Independently verified, and the conservative floor of what we see.
