About Monterey

Runabouts, bowriders and sport boats with more than forty years of legacy

Ever since emerging onto the marine scene more than forty years ago, Monterey has supplied boaters with watercraft for every adventure. Each boat pairs sleek lines and luxurious finishes with real performance, and Monterey has been recognized with honors including Boating Magazine's Best of 2011 and the NMMA CSI Award for eighteen consecutive years.
