About Centurion

Wakeboard and wakesurf boats built for the North Carolina boater

When it comes to maximizing your time on the water, you want a durable and reliable boat. That's why we keep a steady fleet of Centurion boats at Wake & Water. Centurion builds every wake surf and wakeboard boat with the serious rider in mind: advanced wave technology and balanced ballast create larger, longer-lasting waves, and customizable vinyl and flooring make each boat your own.
