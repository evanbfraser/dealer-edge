Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our trade-in process

What information do you need to value my boat?

We need basic information about your boat including year, make, model, condition, engine hours, and location. Photos are helpful but not required for an initial valuation. Our team will schedule an in-person inspection to finalize the offer.

How long does the valuation process take?

Most valuations are completed within 24-48 hours of receiving your information. Once we review your submission, a member of our team will contact you with a preliminary valuation and schedule a time for an in-person inspection if needed.

Do I have to buy a boat from you to trade in mine?

While we strongly encourage applying your trade-in value toward a new or pre-owned boat from our inventory, we can discuss outright purchase options as well. Contact us to learn more about your specific situation.

What if I still owe money on my current boat?

No problem! We work with customers who still have loans on their boats every day. We'll work with your lender to pay off the remaining balance and apply any equity toward your new purchase. Our finance team makes the process seamless.

What types of boats do you accept for trade-in?

We accept a wide variety of boats for trade-in, including ski boats, wakeboard boats, pontoons, fishing boats, and more. Even if you don't see your boat type in our current inventory, we may still be interested. Submit your information and we'll let you know!

Still Have Questions?

Our team is here to help. Call or email us anytime to discuss your trade-in and get expert advice on maximizing your boat's value.
