  1. Centurion

    Wake & surf towboats

    Centurion builds every wake surf and wakeboard boat for riders who want more from their time on the water. Advanced wave technology and balanced ballast make bigger, longer-lasting waves, and customizable vinyl and flooring make each boat your own.

    View Centurion Inventory

  2. Supreme

    Fuel-efficient wake & surf boats

    Superior fuel efficiency, surf technology and torque have made Supreme one of the fastest-growing boat builders in the country. Our team knows the lineup inside and out and will match you with the right model, new or used.

    View Supreme Inventory

  3. Monterey

    Runabouts & bowriders

    For more than forty years Monterey has built boats for every kind of day on the water, pairing sleek lines and luxurious finishes with real performance. It is a fan favorite and an eighteen-time NMMA CSI Award winner.

    View Monterey Inventory

  4. REVEL

    Luxury pontoons

    REVEL pontoons bring a new level of sophistication to Lake Norman, with smooth handling, responsive control and layouts built for time together. Our factory-trained technicians keep them running long after the sale.

    View REVEL Inventory

  5. Fliteboard

    Electric hydrofoils

    Flite eFoils let you glide silently above the water with zero emissions. They are stable enough for first-timers and thrilling for seasoned surfers, and a whole new way to explore the lake.

    View Fliteboard Inventory
