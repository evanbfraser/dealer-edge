Expert Care for Every Boat

Our team services all boat types, including watersports, recreational and fishing boats. From engine repair and gel coat repair to comprehensive conditioning, we handle everything to restore your boat to its best. Our mechanics use advanced diagnostics to pinpoint and resolve any issue, so you get your boat back looking and feeling brand new.

Your engine plays a crucial role in your boat's overall performance, so choosing a trustworthy dealership for repairs matters. We use genuine parts for all repair and replacement work. Whatever the brand, type or model, our skilled mechanics deliver engine service that keeps your boat running at its best.
