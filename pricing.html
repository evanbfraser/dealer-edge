The platform

Everything runs on the same data.
The unit, the buyer and the conversation.

See which capabilities each package includes in Compare packages.

Fast, mobile-first website

Your website and CMS, with AI chat, saved favorites, side-by-side comparison of up to three units, financing calculators, texting and showing requests.

Inventory from your DMS

Units and updates flow in from your DMS. Specs, options, imagery and listing copy get optimized, then syndicated to Boat Trader, RV Trader and Facebook Marketplace.

AI website chat

The AI Sales Captain helps buyers find and compare units, answers their questions and books showings on your salespeople’s calendars, 24/7.

AI-enabled CRM

One record per buyer: the unit, the lead source and the whole conversation. Leads are validated and enriched, and OEM leads enter the same process.

Fast response on every channel

New inquiries answered in under 90 seconds, 24/7. One conversation across SMS, email, chat, phone and DMs, so buyers never start over.

24/7 AI phone answering

Overflow and after-hours sales and service calls answered. The AI answers questions, books the showing or transfers the caller to your team.

Showings on real calendars

AI qualifies the buyer and books the showing on the right salesperson’s actual calendar, so your team shows up to sell, not chase.

Follow-up & re-engagement

Quiet leads keep getting relevant follow-up based on their interests, their timing and whether the unit they wanted is still available.

Post-purchase communication

Once a unit sells, the conversation switches from sales follow-up to review requests and service reminders.

SEO and GEO

Inventory pages and content that help buyers find your dealership in Google and in AI search like ChatGPT and Gemini.

Inventory advertising

Your actual units promoted on Google and Meta, with local targeting and retargeting of engaged visitors. When a unit sells, the ads know.

Social content & unit reels

Posts and unit reels made from your inventory’s photos and specs, for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Newsletter Studio

Personalized newsletters built from each buyer’s interests, the units they viewed and their recent activity.

Analytics & reporting

A current view of lead status, conversations and marketing performance, with attribution where tracking is available.

Dedicated account manager

A real person who knows your dealership, watches your results and is there when you need them.
